Postmortem: cache stampede

Summary

Release 4.2 changed the cache expiry from a sliding window to a fixed one. Every product page expired at the same second, and the database served them all at once. Checkout slowed down for 22 minutes for a quarter of our customers.

What went well

  • The staged deploy kept three quarters of our customers on the old build.
  • The rollback took nine minutes, as the runbook says it should.

What we change

  • Cache expiry gets random jitter of up to a minute.
  • The load test runs with a cold cache as well as a warm one.
  • We practise a rollback every quarter, not only when we need one.
